Pix changed Brazilian e-commerce: instant, free transfers available 24/7. Integrating it into our checkout meant designing for a payment that completes in seconds — or never.

The interesting problems are UX problems: how long to poll for confirmation, when to let the user leave the screen, how to make a copied QR code feel trustworthy. We settled on optimistic navigation with push-notification confirmation as the safety net.
