Most React Native accessibility guides stop at accessibilityLabel. Real screen-reader usability lives in the details: grouping related elements, managing focus after navigation, and announcing dynamic changes.

Working on apps used by healthcare professionals pushed us to test with TalkBack and VoiceOver as part of the definition of done — not as an audit afterthought. The difference is dramatic.
